# SourceIQ — Product Strategy v0 (current concept)

1. **ICP**: Procurement teams at companies running sourcing events.
2. **Killer workflow**: Describe what you need → AI discovers suppliers globally
   → sends anonymous RFIs → tracks responses in a funnel → shortlist.
3. **Simplicity promise**: No supplier database to maintain; AI finds them.
4. **Monetization**: SaaS subscription (Pro plan) via Stripe, with a trial.
5. **Wedge & moat**: AI-native supplier discovery + automated outreach, cheaper
   and faster than analyst-driven sourcing.
6. **Proof / first wow**: A ranked long-list of real suppliers minutes after
   describing the need.
